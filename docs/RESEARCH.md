# Independent non-commercial research

OpenPlugin is an independent, non-commercial research project developed with OpenAI Codex. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or certified by Akai Professional or inMusic. AI assistance and passing tests are not an independent audit.

We explore accessible discovery and management of community instruments and plugins. No sales, paid access, advertising, affiliate links or commercial support promise. Brand names describe compatibility only; original project artwork does not use official Akai logos.

Firmware application sources belong to their owners and are not included. Users provide their own lawfully obtained original images. Redistribution rights must be assessed separately before public firmware distribution.

No activation, signature, licensing or security checks are patched out. Unknown image trailer/signature records fail closed. A candidate passing structural checks is not proven flashable. Real-device validation remains outstanding.
